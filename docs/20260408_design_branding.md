## Name and meaning

*What does "WOT" stand for or evoke? Is the name itself part of the brand, or just an internal codename?*

WOT is not meant to be a super serious brand name. Could be use as wordplay: "Do you even WOT bro?" instead of "Do you even lift bro?" or "You WOT mate??" (Ausie accent).

The core aim of wot is to give people control over their training load. Make it super easy to log any activity and get insight into their training load.
